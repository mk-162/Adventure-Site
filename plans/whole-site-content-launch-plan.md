# Whole Site Content Launch Plan

**Date:** May 2026  
**Status:** Pre-Launch Content Preparation  
**Goal:** Bring all content on Adventure Wales to a launch-ready standard with high editorial quality, accurate information, and professional imagery (no AI images).

---

## 1. Current Phase & Mindset

We are **not** yet running the premium lure sales model.  
The immediate goal is to make the site good enough to launch publicly with confidence.

However, all research and content creation should still follow the quality bar defined in the **Premium Lure Model**, so that when we move into sales mode later, the foundation is already strong.

**Key Rule:** No AI-generated images on any live page.

---

## 2. Image Sourcing Policy (Strict)

### Approved Sources (in order of preference)
1. **Unsplash** – Primary source for now
2. Operator-provided images (when available)
3. Official tourism / destination images with proper attribution
4. High-quality public domain or Creative Commons images (with attribution)

### Rules
- Every image must be manually reviewed.
- Run reverse image search on all Unsplash images to check for overuse.
- No AI-generated or AI-enhanced images.
- Every image must have proper alt text and attribution where required.
- Hero images should feel authentic and relevant to the activity/location.

### Location & Visual Accuracy (Critical Rule)
**All images must visually match the specific place being described.**

- Do not use generic or similar-looking stock photos from other regions.
- Example: A photo of canoeing on a wide, calm river with different geology should **not** be used for canoeing in Snowdonia (which has distinct mountainous, rocky, and often darker water characteristics).
- For activity pages in a specific region, the landscape, terrain, vegetation, and lighting should feel correct for that area.
- When in doubt, prioritise photos that clearly show recognisable Welsh landscapes over more "beautiful" but incorrect ones.
- For operator lure pages, try to source images that reflect the actual location and setting of that business where possible.

### Image Standards by Content Type

| Content Type       | Minimum Images | Notes |
|--------------------|----------------|-------|
| Premium Lure Pages | 8+             | At least 3 showing real people doing the activity |
| Itineraries        | 1 per major stop | Mix of activity + location shots |
| Region Pages       | 10–15          | Strong hero + variety across activities |
| Guides             | 8–12           | Relevant to the topic |
| Events             | 4–6            | Action shots preferred |

## 4. Quality Standards by Content Type

### Operators
- Rewrite all stub descriptions to editorial standard
- Add real offers where possible
- Ensure accurate location and contact details
- Minimum 6–8 good images (Unsplash allowed for now)

### Itineraries
- Minimum 8–12 stops
- Realistic timing and transport between stops
- Weather contingencies included
- Strong visual rhythm
- Clear booking paths

### Region Pages
- Comprehensive but scannable
- Strong local knowledge and practical advice
- Good internal linking to operators and itineraries
- Minimum 1200–1500 words

### Images (All Content)
- No AI images
- Prefer Unsplash with manual review
- Descriptive alt text on every image
- Hero images must feel relevant and high quality

---

## 5. SEO Foundations for Launch

- All pages must follow `directory-seo-standards`
- Implement schema markup on key page types (LocalBusiness, HowTo, FAQPage, Article)
- Strong internal linking structure (Region hubs → Itineraries & Operators)
- Unique, descriptive titles and meta descriptions
- Avoid thin content — minimum word counts must be respected

---

## 6. Research & Data Capture (Future Lure Preparation)

While doing research for launch content, also capture the following where possible (for future sales use):

- Operator contact emails
- Real offers or packages with pricing
- Accreditations
- Strong customer review quotes
- Seasonal notes and restrictions
- Transport and parking details

This data will make the transition to the lure model much faster later.

---

## 7. Workflow

1. **Image Audit** — Go through existing content and flag/replace poor or AI-looking images first.
2. **Content Sprints** — Work by content type (start with Operators and Itineraries).
3. **Quality Gate** — Every page must pass image rules + content standards before publishing.
4. **SEO Check** — Run each major page through the SEO standards checklist.
5. **Track Progress** — Use a simple content tracker (Google Sheet) with status columns.

---

## 8. Success Criteria for Launch

- No AI images anywhere on the live site
- All major region and itinerary pages feel complete and useful
- Strong visual consistency across the site
- Content passes basic SEO and quality checks
- Site feels trustworthy and professional enough to launch publicly

---
