# Adventure Wales — Strategy (Updated May 2026)

## Core Business Model: Pay-to-Play Directory with Premium Content Lures

**Fundamental Shift:** We do **not** offer free meaningful listings. Most operators start as minimal stubs. Real value (detailed profiles, photos, offers, featured placement, leads) lives behind a paywall. We use pre-built premium pages as high-conversion sales assets sent via email.

### Why This Model
- Free listings devalue the platform and attract low-quality operators.
- Pre-built premium pages create instant perceived value ("this already exists for you").
- Outbound + email works better than hoping operators find and claim us.
- Big players are the exception — we give them full treatment to build credibility and traffic, then upsell marketing services once we have audience.

## The Two-Tier Reality

### Tier 1: Stub Listings (Public, Free, Minimal)
- Name + location + basic category only
- No photos, no description, no contact
- "This operator is listed on Adventure Wales" — that's it
- Purpose: Signal that we exist and are building a serious directory

### Tier 2: Premium Listings (Paid, High Value)
- Full rich profile with photos, description, offers, booking links
- Featured in itineraries and search
- Lead forwarding + analytics
- "Verified" or featured badges
- These are the pages we pre-build and send as lures

### Exception: Major Players (Full Details – Strategic Free)
Big destination-draw operators (Zip World, Surf Snowdonia, etc.) get full premium treatment from day one. 
Reasons:
- They are the reason people come to Wales
- Full details increase trust and traffic for the whole platform
- Once we have traffic, we sell them additional marketing services (sponsored articles, homepage features, email blasts, etc.)

## Revenue Model

1. **Primary: Premium Operator Subscriptions**
   - £99–£299/mo depending on tier and prominence
   - Sold via personalised email outreach with pre-built premium page attached/linked

2. **Secondary: Affiliate & Booking Commissions**
   - Booking.com deep links on accommodation
   - Direct booking widgets (future)

3. **Tertiary: Marketing Services for Big Players**
   - Sponsored content
   - Email features
   - Homepage takeovers
   - Performance marketing management

## Content Play: Premium Pages as Sales Assets

### The Lure Mechanism
1. Research identifies high-potential operators (volume + quality).
2. We pre-build a beautiful, complete premium page for them (even though they haven't paid yet).
3. We email the owner with a personalised message:
   > "We've built your full profile on Adventure Wales. Here's what it looks like live. Would you like to claim it and go live?"
4. The page acts as both proof of quality and FOMO.

### Content Standards for Premium Lures
- Professional photography (or high-quality sourced images with attribution)
- Compelling, benefit-focused description
- Real offers or packages highlighted
- Integration into 1–2 relevant itineraries
- Clear ROI language ("X bookings per month at this price point")
- Easy upgrade/claim CTA

### Content Calendar Focus
- 60% of editorial effort goes into making premium lure pages look exceptional
- 30% into big-player pages (to drive traffic)
- 10% into general journal / SEO content

## Positioning
Adventure Wales is the **only** serious, high-quality adventure directory in Wales that operators have to earn the right to appear on properly. We are not a free-for-all listings site.
