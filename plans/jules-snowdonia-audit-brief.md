# Jules Research Brief: Snowdonia Content Completeness Audit

## Objective
Audit the Snowdonia region and its key Activity × Region pages to assess how complete and authoritative the content currently feels.

## Scope
Focus on:
- Snowdonia region page
- Major Activity × Region pages (e.g. Coasteering in Snowdonia, Mountain Biking in Snowdonia, Hiking in Snowdonia, etc.)

## Tasks

1. Review each page and give a **completeness score** (1–5), where:
   - 5 = Feels very complete and authoritative
   - 3 = Average coverage
   - 1 = Clearly missing important content

2. For each page, identify:
   - What Snowdonia (or the specific activity) is **most famous for**
   - Any **major missing spots, operators, or experiences**
   - Specific gaps that should be addressed

3. Update the file `content/inventory/coverage-findings.csv` with your findings.

## Guidelines
- Be specific and practical.
- Prioritise quality and depth over speed.
- Focus on what would make the site feel genuinely comprehensive to a visitor.

## Output
Structured updates to the CSV tracker with clear findings and scores.
