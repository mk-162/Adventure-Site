# Adventure Wales Commercial Offerings Plan (Updated May 2026)

**Document Version:** 2.0  
**Date:** May 2026  
**Key Change:** Shift from freemium to "no free meaningful listings" model. Premium pages are pre-built as sales lures. Big players receive full treatment strategically.

---

## 1. New Business Model Summary

We do **not** give away valuable listings. Most operators begin as minimal public stubs. Real profiles (photos, descriptions, offers, leads, featured placement) are premium-only. We proactively build premium pages and use them as high-conversion email outreach assets.

**Exception:** Major destination-draw operators receive full premium profiles immediately to build platform credibility and traffic, then we upsell additional marketing services.

## 2. Revenue Streams (Revised)

### 2.1 Premium Operator Subscriptions (Primary Revenue)

Sold via outbound email with pre-built premium page as the lure.

| Tier          | Monthly | Annual     | What They Get                              | Target |
|---------------|---------|------------|--------------------------------------------|--------|
| **Premium**   | £149    | £1,490     | Full profile, photos, offers, itinerary features, lead forwarding, analytics | Main sales target |
| **Premium Plus** | £299 | £2,990     | Everything + top placement, sponsored articles, account management | Big players upsell |

**Sales Process:**
1. Research & select target operator
2. Pre-build complete premium page (even if they haven't paid)
3. Send personalised email with link/preview of their live premium page
4. "Would you like to activate this and go live?"

### 2.2 Strategic Free Premium for Big Players

Major operators (Zip World, Surf Snowdonia, etc.) get full profiles at no cost initially.

**Rationale:**
- They drive real visitor intent
- Full details make the platform look authoritative
- Creates traffic we can later monetise through marketing services

**Upsell Path:** Once we have meaningful traffic, sell them sponsored content, email features, performance marketing, etc.

### 2.3 Affiliate & Commission Revenue

- Booking.com integration on accommodation pages
- Future direct booking widgets

### 2.4 Marketing Services (Once Traffic Exists)

Sold primarily to big players who already have strong free presence:
- Sponsored journal articles
- Homepage features
- Email newsletter sponsorships
- Custom itinerary placements

## 3. Content Play: Premium Pages as Sales Assets

### Philosophy
Every premium page we build is both a product and a sales tool. We invest real editorial and visual quality into pages for operators who haven't paid yet, because the quality of the lure determines conversion.

### Premium Page Standards (Lure Quality Bar)
- High-quality, relevant photography (sourced or commissioned)
- Benefit-led, persuasive copy (not just facts)
- Real offers or packages highlighted
- Embedded into 1–2 relevant itineraries
- Clear value proposition ("Expected X–Y qualified leads per month")
- Professional design consistent with the rest of the site

### Content Allocation
- 60% of content effort → Premium lure pages for outreach targets
- 25% of content effort → Big player pages (traffic drivers)
- 15% of content effort → General journal, guides, SEO

### Outreach Volume Target
Start with 20–30 high-quality pre-built premium lure pages per month, sent via personalised email.

## 4. Implementation Priorities

1. **Immediate:** Update all existing plans and goals to reflect "no free valuable listings"
2. **This Month:** Build first batch of 20–30 premium lure pages for top prospects
3. **Ongoing:** Maintain strict quality bar on lure pages so conversion stays high
4. **Later:** Develop marketing services catalogue for big players once traffic justifies it
