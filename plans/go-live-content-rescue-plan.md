Open PR: #105, `Launch prep + content quality overhaul`

## Executive decision

The previous plan was too much like a content expansion roadmap. The launch goal is narrower and more brutal:

1. Hide, noindex, remove, or redirect weak pages.
2. Make the visible launch surface credible.
3. Fix the obvious trust breakers: missing operator data, wrong images, empty activity types, unverified AI-feeling copy.
4. Ship a smaller, cleaner site rather than a large messy directory.

Do not try to complete the whole directory before launch. That will make quality worse.

## Go-live definition

Adventure Wales can go live when these are true:

1. Homepage, region hubs, top Snowdonia pages, directory entry points, and search flows do not expose broken-looking pages.
2. No visible Tier 1 / Tier 2 operator page is missing a description, hero image, logo, website, or basic contact fields unless deliberately marked as unclaimed and noindexed.
3. No homepage, region, combo, or operator hero image is obviously the wrong country or wrong activity.
4. Empty activity type pages are removed, redirected, or noindexed.
5. Snowdonia is strong enough to act as the launch showcase.
6. The site is technically shippable: build passes, sitemap is sane, critical schema/metadata exists, no obvious crawl traps.

## Revised launch strategy

### Phase 0: Stop the bleeding, before writing more content

Goal: prevent weak content from damaging trust at launch.

Actions:

1. Add a launch visibility rule:
   - `published = true` only for pages that pass the launch gate.
   - Stub or incomplete operators should either be hidden from public lists or rendered with a clear unclaimed state and noindex.
2. Suppress long-tail combo pages that have no enrichment data.
   - Current state: 40/492 combo pages have enrichment data.
   - Do not write 452 pages before launch.
   - Route or sitemap should only expose enriched combos plus deliberate regional pages.
3. Remove or noindex empty activity types.
   - Scenic Railway
   - Running
   - Gorge Walking
   - plus the newly flagged fourth empty type from the latest audit
4. Decide redirects for nonsense combos.
   - Snowdonia coasteering should not be padded, because Snowdonia has no proper coasteering coastline.
   - Snowdonia canyoning should merge into gorge-walking unless there is enough distinct verified content.
   - Horse riding in Snowdonia can wait unless it is needed for itinerary completeness.

Definition of done:

- The public navigation, sitemap, and search do not lead users into obvious stubs.
- Audit gaps may still exist in the database, but weak pages are not prominent launch pages.

### Phase 2: Image rescue

Goal: remove the fastest visual signal that the site is low quality.

Known audit issues:

- Homepage hero images are verified Welsh.
- Homepage CTA/newsletter images were previously flagged as wrong: Maryland USA, Poland map, Colorado/Rockies.
- Region images flagged:
  - Snowdonia hero: wrong, Pacific Northwest forest.
  - Wye Valley hero: wrong, New Zealand alpine lake.
- Combo image issues flagged:
  - Snowdonia mountain biking: generic forest, no MTB.
  - Gower surfing: Rhossili location but no surfers.
  - Snowdonia hiking: location OK but no hikers, marginal.
- Historical replacement log shows many earlier non-Wales images were overwritten, but attribution file was not fully updated.

Rules:

- No AI-generated images for real places, operators, or events.
- Region images must show the exact region, not generic Wales.
- Combo images must show both the right activity and the right place where possible.
- Operator images must come from allowed/licensed sources or be withheld until verified.

Definition of done:

- Homepage, region cards, top combo pages, and top operator pages have no obviously wrong-country/wrong-activity images.
- Attribution is updated for any new image.

### Phase 3: Snowdonia launch showcase

Goal: Snowdonia becomes the proof that the site is worth using.

Already strong or near-strong:

- Snowdonia region page: score 5.
- Hiking, mountain biking, climbing, caving, gorge walking, zip-lining, wild swimming, trail running: score 5 in tracker.

Still needs launch verification:

- Surfing: score 3, verify Adventure Parc / Surf Snowdonia branding, pricing, lodging detail.
- Underground trampolines: score 3, verify current Zip World pricing, age/height restrictions.
- Scenic railway: score 3, verify current-year timetables, closures, ticket bands.
- Kayaking: score 3, verify river grades and current local hire/operator details.
- Beaches: score 3, verify Black Rock Sands rules, lifeguard dates, parking.

Editorial rule:

- Do not include live pricing/opening/timetable details unless verified from current official sources.
- If not verified, write evergreen practical copy and link users to the operator for current details.

Definition of done:

- Every Snowdonia page in launch navigation is either score 4-5, or hidden/redirected.
- Verification flags are resolved or copy avoids making time-sensitive claims.

### Phase 4: Technical SEO launch gate

Goal: do not launch crawlable junk.

Known issues from audits:

- Severe duplicate intent risk around activity hubs, activity listings, region combos, and search results.
- `/search?activity=*` was previously flagged as broken/empty or duplicate-prone.
- Hub pages may not be represented properly in sitemap depending on current implementation.
- Dynamic metadata needs improvement on several dynamic page types.
- Directory pages need LocalBusiness schema where data exists.

Actions:

1. Sitemap should include only canonical, launch-worthy URLs.
2. Search/filter result pages should be noindex unless they are intentionally built landing pages.
3. Activity type listing pages should not compete with richer hub or region pages.
4. Add canonical tags for overlapping page patterns.
5. Check rendered schema in browser, not only with curl.
6. Build and typecheck before merge.

Definition of done:

- Sitemap contains no obvious stubs.
- Search pages are not index traps.
- Canonical hierarchy is deliberate.

### Phase 5: Events, journal, and long tail after launch

Goal: avoid delaying launch with low-leverage cleanup.

Events are the largest audit category: 1,179 gaps. Most are medium/low thin-description or missing-detail issues. This is too large for pre-launch.

Launch approach:

- Keep only credible, current, well-known events public at launch.
- Hide, noindex, or defer weak event pages.
- Journal hero image fixes are polish, not core launch blockers unless the articles are featured on homepage/navigation.
- Long-tail combo expansion waits until after launch.

## Launch gate checklist

A page can be public at launch if it passes all relevant checks:

Content:

- Specific to Wales and the exact region/activity.
- No generic AI travel filler.
- No unverifiable safety, pricing, availability, or timetable claims.
- Has useful local/practical detail.
- Has a clear next action.

Data:

- Operator fields complete if the page is commercial.
- No duplicate slug confusion.
- Region/activity mappings correct.
- Empty activity types not exposed.

Images:

- Real photograph.
- Correct location/activity/operator.
- Licensed or source logged.
- Sensible alt text.

SEO:

- Unique title and meta description.
- Canonical URL set.
- In sitemap only if launch-worthy.
- Schema valid where applicable.

Technical:

- Typecheck passes.
- Build passes.
- No critical browser errors on homepage, search, Snowdonia, top combo, top operator.

## What not to do now

- Do not bulk-generate hundreds of combo pages.
- Do not publish operator stubs just to make the directory look bigger.
- Do not use AI images.
- Do not spend launch time on low-visibility journal/event polish unless those pages are featured.
- Do not chase every audit item equally. The audit is a risk map, not a launch checklist.
