## Why this operator matters

Adventure Parc Snowdonia operates **the only inland surf lagoon of its scale in the UK** — a purpose-built wave-generating lake in a mountain valley. It opened in 2015 as "Surf Snowdonia" (the world's first inland surf venue of this kind at the time) and was rebranded to Adventure Parc Snowdonia as the wider activity hub developed around it. There are now a handful of similar inland-wave venues globally (The Wave near Bristol, URBNSURF in Melbourne, BSR in Texas) but in the UK this remains effectively the only operation at this scale, and the only one inside a National Park.

3. It's positioned next to the Snowdon massif, which makes it part of every multi-activity Eryri itinerary (surf in the morning, summit in the afternoon).

## Experience breakdown — the venue as a whole

Adventure Parc Snowdonia is no longer just a surf lagoon; it's a multi-activity hub built around the wave. The current product split:

### The Surf Lagoon (the headline product)

- A long, narrow concrete lake with a mechanical wave-generating system. Produces a consistent shoulder-high wave every 90 seconds approximately, **both sides of a central pier** — so left-handers and right-handers run simultaneously.
- **Three wave grades** in sequence: a beginner inside-bay rolling whitewater wave, an intermediate peeling shoulder, and an advanced bigger-set wave for proficient surfers. You book the grade that matches your ability.
- **Sessions are timed** (typically 1 hour on the water) and graded by experience level. Equipment included.
- Runs year-round; heated wetsuits available in colder months.

### The Adventure Hub

The non-surf side of the venue — built out as a complementary activity park:

- **Ninja assault course** — multi-element obstacle course over water; a Saturday-morning TV obstacle-course format adapted for visitors.
- **Pump track** — outdoor concrete pump track for bikes and skateboards.
- **Crash & Splash** — inflatable water-park course on the lagoon when conditions allow.
- Climbing wall, soft play, mini-golf and other smaller-scale family activities depending on season.

### On-site accommodation

- **Hilton Garden Inn Snowdonia** — branded hotel on the lagoon perimeter; verify current branding (the on-site hotel has been through a rebrand and may now use a different operator).
- **Glamping pods** in the lagoon-side field.
- **Camping pitches** for tents and small vans.

The accommodation is the operator's surf-package play — visitors can roll out of bed and walk to their session, which is one of the few places in the UK that's true.

## Durable framing facts

- **First inland surf lagoon of its kind in the world** (2015 opening, then trading as Surf Snowdonia). Several similar venues have opened globally since, but this was the original at this scale.
- **Inside Snowdonia National Park** (Dolgarrog, Conwy Valley) — the only major adventure venue inside the park boundary on this scale.
- **Only one of its kind in the UK at this scale.** The Wave near Bristol opened in 2019 using a different wave-generation technology; Adventure Parc Snowdonia remains the only mountain-valley inland surf venue in the UK.
- **Wave technology** — Wavegarden Cove or related lagoon-tech (verify current generation system with operator before claiming specifics).

## Pairing notes

- **Pair with hiking days in the Carneddau** — Dolgarrog sits at the foot of the Carneddau range, with Llyn Eigiau and the Aber Falls trailhead within 20 minutes. This is the natural "surf morning, hike afternoon" combo.
- **Pair with the Ogwen Valley** — 25 minutes drive south for Cwm Idwal, Tryfan, Glyder Fach.
- **Pair with the Conwy Valley railway** — visitors arriving by train can use the Conwy Valley line direct to Dolgarrog (or the closer Tal-y-Cafn station).
- **Sea-surf alternative** — the closest open-Atlantic surf to Adventure Parc is Hell's Mouth on the Llŷn Peninsula (~1h15) and Cable Bay on Anglesey (~1h). Both already cross-linked from the [Snowdonia surfing combo](/snowdonia/things-to-do/surfing).
