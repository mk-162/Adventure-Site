# Growth Playbook (researched 2026-07-06, pre-launch)

Context for agents with none: Adventure Wales (adventurewales.co.uk) is a brand-new Next.js trip-planning site + operator directory for Welsh outdoor adventure. Launch scope: 8 regions, 24 region+activity combo pages, 18 verified operators. Monetization: operator tiers (free / £9.99 / £29.99 per month), Booking.com affiliate for accommodation, enquiry forwarding.

## 1. SEO for local/travel directories in 2026

**Programmatic SEO is high-risk at scale, fine at our scale.** Google's [May 2026 core update explicitly targeted templated programmatic pages](https://www.1clickreport.com/blog/google-may-2026-core-update-programmatic-seo-dead) — city×service template-fill pages where variable content is <20% of the page are being demoted *sitewide*, not just page-by-page. What survives: pages with proprietary/filterable data users can sort and interact with, and roughly [1,500–2,500 words with at least one piece of information that exists nowhere else](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/surviving-march-2026-core-update-seo-recovery-strategies) (own data, methodology, verified operator details). Our 24 combo pages are safely below spam-scale **if each has unique verified-operator data, real local detail, and interactive filtering** — do not expand to hundreds of thin combos.

**E-E-A-T now explicitly covers travel.** The [December 2025 core update extended heightened E-E-A-T requirements into travel content, and March 2026 amplified first-hand Experience above other signals](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/e-e-a-t-march-2026-google-rewards-experience-content-guide). Concrete signals that moved rankings: original photography with EXIF/location metadata, dated visit notes ("visited 12 Mar 2026"), named authors with structured author pages and consistent bylines. For us: the operator-verification process *is* an E-E-A-T asset — surface "verified in person / by phone on [date]" on listings; avoid stock photos on combo pages.

**Structured data:** [TouristAttraction/TouristDestination/TouristTrip are valid and help Google understand pages but trigger no rich result; Product, Event, and Hotel schema do](https://mapatlas.eu/solutions/guides/travel-tourism). Practical stack: `Product`+`Offer`+`AggregateRating` on operator/activity pages (star ratings + price badges in SERPs), `TouristTrip` with `itinerary` on multi-day trip plans, `TouristAttraction` as `additionalType`, plus `FAQPage` and `BreadcrumbList`. Include `tourBookingPage` where operators take bookings.

**Ranking timeline for "coasteering pembrokeshire"-type terms:** realistic. For low-competition long-tail on a technically sound new site, [first rankings in 4–8 weeks and page-1 for specific long-tail queries in 30–90 days](https://resources.averi.ai/benchmarks/seo-ranking-timeline); [local rankings stabilize at 6–12 months](https://www.kexworks.com/seo-tips/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank-locally/), and [new domains (<12 months) should add 2–3 months for the trust-building phase](https://www.searchscaleai.com/blog/how-long-does-seo-take-realistic-timeline-2026/). Expect: impressions month 1–2, long-tail clicks month 2–4, meaningful positions on head combo terms month 6–12. Head terms like "coasteering pembrokeshire" have entrenched operators + Visit Wales ranking — win the modifier space first ("coasteering pembrokeshire stag do", "coasteering near st davids family").

**AI Overviews change the math.** AIOs now appear on [~48% of Google queries (Mar 2026), up from 31% in early 2025](https://thestacc.com/blog/google-ai-overview-statistics/); organic CTR drops [~61% when an AIO is present](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/google-ai-overviews-the-end-of-traditional-ctr-and-how-to-adapt-in-2025) and a field study measured [38% fewer outbound clicks on triggered queries](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-overviews-cut-organic-clicks-38-field-study-finds/573145/). Implications: (a) informational "what is coasteering" content earns citations not clicks — write it to be cited (clear entity definitions, stats, schema) and treat it as brand exposure; (b) prioritize transactional/local intent ("coasteering providers in X", price/availability data) where users still click through; (c) [Reddit is ~21% of AI Overview cited sources](https://content-whale.com/us/blog/reddit-search-growth-2026-brand-strategy/), so forum presence feeds AI visibility too. Track GSC impressions, not just clicks — impressions understate AI-citation exposure.

## 2. Non-SEO channels that fit this product

- **Pinterest** — best fit for trip-planning: [pins generate traffic for years vs ephemeral IG/TikTok, and travel inspiration is a core Pinterest category](https://improvado.io/blog/pinterest-marketing-tactics). Make one vertical pin per combo page + per itinerary; keyword the boards ("Wales adventure holidays", "Snowdonia itinerary"). Low ongoing cost, compounding.
- **TikTok/Instagram Reels** — [POV/UGC clips of real activities are the strongest format; small, surprising destinations can outperform big ones](https://vidlo.video/blog/tiktok-travel-trends-2026/). We don't run activities, so the play is **repost/collab with the 18 operators' footage** (they have GoPro content; offer to edit it for them — doubles as a premium-tier perk). Caveat: [only ~5% of travelers say social trends primarily drive their decisions in 2026](https://creators.yahoo.com/lifestyle/story/forget-tiktok-travel-trends--heres-whats-actually-inspiring-trips-in-2026-211543367.html) — treat as awareness, not conversion.
- **Google Business Profile** — mostly indirect for us: a directory without a public premises won't rank in the local pack ([GBP signals are ~32% of local-pack weight; 93% of local searches trigger a map pack](https://biziq.com/blog/google-business-profile-statistics/)). The play: **help operators optimize their GBPs** (primary category, photos, review velocity — [complete profiles get 7x more clicks](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/google-business-profile-guide-every-feature-2026)) as a paid-tier service, and get adventurewales.co.uk listed as the website/booking link where the operator agrees.
- **Email** — own the audience AI search can't intermediate. Travel newsletters open at [~22–30%](https://www.mailerlite.com/blog/compare-your-email-performance-metrics-industry-benchmarks); niche + [segmented/behavior-based flows dramatically outperform generic blasts](https://www.brevo.com/blog/email-marketing-benchmarks/). Lead magnet: downloadable region itineraries / "Wales adventure weekend planner" gated by email on combo pages.
- **Partnerships** — [Visit Wales product listing is free](https://industry.visitwales.com/supporting-you/managing-your-visit-wales-tourism-product-listing) (quality-assured businesses auto-listed; contact visitwalessupport@granicus.com). Also: check operators hold [AAAS (Adventure Activity Assurance Scheme) accreditation](http://www.visitwales.com/wato/requirements-of-a-visit-wales-approved-adventure-tourism-operator) — a verification/badge angle. Approach YHA hostels (Snowdonia/Pembrokeshire), university outdoor clubs, and MWT Cymru for links/cross-promotion. [Beyonk's partner network](https://beyonk.com/for-partners/) distributes bookable UK experiences to tourism-promoter sites — a route to real-time availability + booking revenue without building it.
- **Reddit/forums** — [Reddit threads with 100+ upvotes can index in Google within 1–2 days and Reddit is heavily cited in AI Overviews](https://content-whale.com/us/blog/reddit-search-growth-2026-brand-strategy/). Participate genuinely in r/Wales, r/Snowdonia, r/UKhiking, r/CampingandHiking UK threads; answer "what should we do in Pembrokeshire" questions with real detail, occasional link. Never astroturf.
- **Digital PR** — [niche data-driven campaigns with simple methodology get travel links; interactive data resources journalists cite (e.g. FlightAware's MiseryMap) work best; every trail/region is a localizable angle](https://greenflagdigital.com/travel-digital-pr-campaign-examples/). Ideas from our data: "Wales adventure price index" (avg price per activity per region from operator data), seasonal water-temperature/coasteering-conditions tracker, "most in-demand adventures" from enquiry stats. Hooks exist: [Eryri visits +59% since 2019, winter adventure visits +63%, coasteering invented near St Davids](https://www.wales247.co.uk/how-wales-became-one-of-europes-fastest-growing-destinations-for-outdoor-tourism) — Welsh regional press (WalesOnline, Wales247) is receptive.

## 3. Monetization benchmarks

- **Free→paid conversion:** the honest benchmark is the freemium SaaS range — [2–5% of free users convert, with a quarter of products under 2.5%](https://www.withdaydream.com/library/insights/freemium-conversion-rate); [tightly targeted high-intent B2B tools reach 5–15%](https://userpilot.com/blog/freemium-to-premium/). Directory-specific longitudinal data is thin — [verified ROI benchmarks are a documented gap](https://www.jasminedirectory.com/blog/directory-listings-vs-paid-ads-where-to-invest-for-local-growth/). With 18 hand-recruited operators (high-touch, high-intent), 2–4 paid within 90 days (~10–20%) is a fair stretch goal; at pure-funnel rates expect 0–1. **The paid case to operators:** [premium listings see 2–3x CTR and 1.5–2x inquiry conversion vs free](https://turnkeydirectories.com/online-directory-pricing-how-much-charge-listings/).
- **Pricing sanity-check:** guidance for new directory operators is [≤$40/month, ≤$300/year](https://directorist.com/blog/how-to-set-listing-rates-business-directory/). £9.99/£29.99 sits comfortably inside that — not underpriced for a zero-traffic launch, and £29.99 needs demonstrable leads to justify. Anchor with annual pricing (e.g. £99/£299 ≈ 2 months free) once value is proven. The stronger justification for £29.99 is bundled services (GBP optimization, video edits, featured placement) rather than placement alone.
- **Booking.com affiliate:** headline [4% on completed accommodation stays, 6% car rental, 4% attractions](https://affiliates.support.booking.com/kb/s/article/Commission-and-Payments) — but note it's structured as [25–40% of Booking.com's ~15% hotel commission, so effective take is roughly 3.75–6% of booking value](https://reacheffect.com/blog/how-much-does-booking-com-pay-affiliates/), and paid only on *completed stays* (cancellations kill ~20–30%). Realistic scale: [~50k monthly visitors ≈ 20–30 bookings ≈ $120–180/month](https://reacheffect.com/blog/how-much-does-booking-com-pay-affiliates/). Published EPC data for accommodation content is thin; travel content converts at [~1–3%](https://ecomobi.com/join-booking-com-affiliate-on-ecomobi-passio/). Treat as pennies until five-figure monthly traffic — do not build launch financials on it.
- **Activity affiliates:** [Viator 8% (to ~12% at volume) and GetYourGuide 8% base, both 30-day cookies](https://www.sambahq.com/ota-supplier-guide/ota-commission-rates) — useful fallback monetization for activities whose operators aren't on our directory, but conflicts with the operator-subscription value prop (sending "their" demand to OTAs); use sparingly for gaps. UK-native [Beyonk charges operators ~4% booking fee, partner-network distribution at 5–10% commission, and pays 25% revenue share for referring operators to its software](https://beyonk.com/for-partners/) — the most aligned option: operator keeps a direct-feeling booking, we earn on distribution/referral.
- **Lead-gen pricing (future tier):** [tour/activity operators pay $10–50 per lead; adventure tour operators specifically $15–50 per enquiry](https://www.causalfunnel.com/blog/what-is-cost-per-lead-cpl-for-travel-businesses-complete-2025-guide/), with travel CPL averaging [$106 and rising](https://www.causalfunnel.com/blog/what-is-cost-per-lead-cpl-for-travel-businesses-complete-2025-guide/). At even £10–15/qualified enquiry, ~3 enquiries/month outvalues the £29.99 tier — count forwarded enquiries from day 1; that data reprices the tiers later.

## 4. 90-day post-launch growth plan

**Weeks 1–2 — plumbing + baseline.** Submit sitemap to GSC + Bing WMT; verify all 24 combo pages + 18 operator pages request indexing. Ship `Product`/`Offer`/`FAQPage`/`BreadcrumbList` schema and validate in Rich Results Test. Register the Visit Wales product listing; email all 18 operators asking them to add adventurewales.co.uk link from their sites (18 relevant DR-anchor backlinks is the single cheapest authority win available). Create Pinterest account + 8 region boards, pin every combo page. Set up plausible enquiry tracking per operator. *Metrics: 100% of ~60 core pages indexed; GSC baseline recorded; ≥6 operator backlinks live.*
**Month 1 — E-E-A-T content + first channels.** Add "verified on [date]" + original photography to all operator listings; author page for the site owner. Publish 6–8 long-tail experience-led posts targeting modifier queries ("coasteering pembrokeshire hen party", "beginner canyoning snowdonia") at the [20–40 low-competition-post cadence recommended for new domains](https://www.seospace.co/blog/how-long-does-seo-take). Launch newsletter with an itinerary lead magnet on combo pages. Start genuine Reddit participation (2–3 quality answers/week). Send every operator their first "your listing performance" email — this is the upgrade-nurture flow. *Metrics: GSC impressions >1,000/wk trending up; first non-brand clicks; 50+ email subscribers; every operator has received ≥1 stats email.*
**Months 2–3 — links, PR, first revenue.** Run one data-driven digital PR piece from our own data (e.g. Wales adventure price index) pitched to WalesOnline/Wales247/UK outdoor press. Approach YHA + 2 outdoor clubs for partnership links. Pilot the £9.99 pitch to the 5 operators with the most forwarded enquiries, with their enquiry stats as the proof ([premium = 2–3x CTR](https://turnkeydirectories.com/online-directory-pricing-how-much-charge-listings/) framing); offer founding-member annual pricing. Evaluate Beyonk partner integration for bookable availability. Double down on whichever of Pinterest/Reddit/email shows referral traffic; kill the rest. *Metrics: ≥1 PR link from DR50+ site; 10+ referring domains total; GSC clicks 500+/month; ≥25 enquiries forwarded; **first paid operator upgrade** (target 2–4 by day 90); 200+ email subscribers.*

**Thin-evidence flags:** directory-specific free→paid conversion data is anecdotal (SaaS benchmarks used as proxy); Booking.com EPC for accommodation content has no reliable published figure; "premium listings 2–3x CTR" comes from directory-industry vendors, not neutral studies; 2026 core-update analyses are from SEO agencies interpreting Google, not Google statements.
